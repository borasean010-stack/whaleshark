## Full description (4000 chars max)

See the whale sharks of Libertad, Antique — book your tour in minutes.

Boracay Whale Shark is the official booking app for whale shark tours in
Libertad, Antique, Philippines — the same tours and pricing as
boracaywhaleshark.com, now in your pocket.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Book VIP Fast Track, Fast Track, Regular, or Ticket-Only tours
• Add a Hopping Tour or Land Tour to your whale shark trip
• Pick your tour date, guest count, and meeting time in a few taps
• Choose how you pay: GCash, on-site cash, or visit our office in advance
• Get a confirmation email and push notification the moment your booking
  is confirmed
• Travel agency partners: manage your own bookings, deposit balance, and
  transaction history from the Partner tab

WHY BOOK WITH US
Libertad Whale Shark is the official authorized sales point for whale
shark tours in the area. We work directly with local guides and transport
partners, so your booking goes straight to the source — no middlemen,
no surprises.

MEETING POINT
All tours pick up from Jollibee Main Road, Boracay — you'll see the exact
meeting time and location for your tour type right in the app.

Questions? Reach us through the contact details on
boracaywhaleshark.com.
