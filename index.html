About the Whale Shark

Welcome to the Libertad Whale Shark Experience, home to one of the most unforgettable whale shark encounters in the Philippines. Located in Libertad, Antique, just a short journey from Boracay Island, this experience offers the opportunity to observe the world's largest fish in its natural habitat through responsible and sustainable marine tourism.

20M Max Length (65 ft)
30T Max Weight
0% Danger to Humans
1 of 1 Unique Spot Pattern

The whale shark (Rhincodon typus) is the largest fish on Earth. Despite their enormous size, these gentle giants are completely harmless to humans. Their calm nature and slow, graceful movements make swimming alongside them one of the most remarkable wildlife experiences in the Philippines.

Unlike whales, whale sharks are filter-feeding fish. They feed primarily on plankton, krill, fish eggs, and small marine organisms by filtering thousands of liters of seawater through their wide mouths. Their presence is an important indicator of a healthy marine ecosystem.

One of the whale shark's most distinctive features is its pattern of white spots and stripes. No two whale sharks share the same markings — each is unique, much like a human fingerprint — and scientists use these natural markings to identify and study individual whale sharks throughout their migrations.

Whale sharks are the only living species in the Rhincodontidae family and have inhabited Earth's oceans for millions of years. Today, they migrate through tropical and warm-temperate waters worldwide, including the rich marine ecosystems of the Philippines, where they play an essential role in maintaining ocean biodiversity.

VULNERABLE SPECIES

Conservation Matters

The whale shark is classified as a Vulnerable species due to threats such as fishing bycatch, vessel strikes, marine pollution, and habitat degradation. Protecting these gentle giants helps preserve healthy oceans for future generations.

At the Libertad Whale Shark Experience, conservation comes first. We follow strict environmental guidelines, respect wildlife, and minimize human impact during every encounter — providing an authentic, ethical, and educational experience that supports both marine conservation and the local community.

Because whale sharks are wild, free-ranging animals, sightings can never be guaranteed — making each encounter a genuine privilege and a reminder of the importance of protecting the oceans they call home.

Whether you're visiting Antique, exploring Panay Island, or looking for a whale shark tour near Boracay Island, the Libertad Whale Shark Experience offers an unforgettable opportunity to observe these magnificent creatures responsibly while helping protect one of the Philippines' most extraordinary marine treasures.

Safety & Rules

Protecting gentle giants is the top priority. Please strictly follow these regulations.

DO'S

  • Listen carefully to all instructions from your guides, boat crew, and local authorities.
  • Wear your life jacket and snorkeling equipment properly at all times.
  • Enter and exit the water slowly, calmly, and only when instructed.
  • Maintain a minimum distance of 5 meters from the whale shark's head and 6 meters from its sides and tail.
  • Observe whale sharks quietly and respectfully, allowing them to swim freely in their natural habitat.
  • Use only reef-safe sunscreen or avoid applying sunscreen immediately before the tour whenever possible.
  • Respect all marine life and follow marine park regulations throughout your visit.

DON'TS

  • Do not touch, ride, chase, corner, or block the path of a whale shark.
  • Do not feed whale sharks or throw food, trash, or any objects into the sea.
  • Do not use flash photography, underwater lights, or bright strobes that may disturb wildlife.
  • Do not make loud noises, splash excessively, or behave in a manner that stresses marine life.
  • Do not collect corals, shells, or any marine organisms.
  • Do not jump from the boat unless instructed by your guide.
  • Do not enter the water without permission from the guide or boat crew.
  • 고래상어를 만지거나, 올라타거나, 쫓거나, 몰거나, 이동 경로를 막지 마세요.
  • 고래상어에게 먹이를 주거나 음식·쓰레기·물건을 바다에 던지지 마세요.
  • 야생동물을 방해할 수 있는 플래시 촬영, 수중 조명, 밝은 스트로보 사용을 삼가주세요.
  • 큰 소리를 내거나 과도하게 물을 튀기거나 해양생물에게 스트레스를 줄 수 있는 행동을 하지 마세요.
  • 산호, 조개껍데기 등 해양생물을 채집하지 마세요.
  • 가이드의 지시 없이 보트에서 뛰어들지 마세요.
  • 가이드나 승무원의 허락 없이 입수하지 마세요.

Important Information

  • Whale sharks are wild animals, and sightings cannot be guaranteed.
  • Tours are subject to weather, sea conditions, and marine park regulations.
  • Always follow the instructions of your guide for your safety and the welfare of the whale sharks.
  • Participation is at your own risk.
  • Guests who fail to follow these guidelines or engage in unsafe or harmful behavior may be removed from the tour without refund.
